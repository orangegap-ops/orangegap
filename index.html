Bridging the gaps

About OrangeGap

Our mission

OrangeGap exists to close the gap between what students need and who has it. One platform for buying and selling with people you can trust, discovering campus events, and finding jobs, internships, bursaries and vacation work - all built around your specific campus and faculty.

Our story

OrangeGap started at NWU, built by a student who saw how scattered campus life had become - WhatsApp groups for selling textbooks, Facebook groups for events, noticeboards for jobs, none of it connected or verified. OrangeGap brings all of it into one trusted, campus-first platform.

Questions and answers

Is OrangeGap free to use?

Yes. Signing up, browsing, posting an event and posting an opportunity are free. Listing an item is free, and your Profile shows what you will receive for each sale. Advertising in the Sponsored section costs R10 a week.

How does payment work?

Buyers pay through PayFast, so card details never touch OrangeGap. The money reaches OrangeGap first, and the seller is paid out once the order is sorted.

How long until my listing goes live?

We review every listing before it appears. We aim to review new listings within 24 hours.

Who can use it?

OrangeGap is currently built for students at NWU Potchefstroom. Anyone can sign up with an email address, and you confirm your email once when you register.

What if something is wrong with a listing?

Use the Report button on the listing and tell us what is wrong. The OrangeGap team reviews every report.

Terms of Service

Last updated September 2026.

Using OrangeGap

OrangeGap is a marketplace and listings platform for students, currently built for NWU Potchefstroom. You must give accurate information when you sign up and when you list an item, event or opportunity.

Listings and pricing

Posting a marketplace listing is free. The price shown to buyers includes a service margin that OrangeGap keeps; your Profile shows the amount you will receive for each sale. Advertising in the Sponsored section is a paid, optional feature.

Ownership and lawful goods

You confirm that you lawfully own anything you list, and that it is legal to sell. OrangeGap may ask for evidence of ownership and can remove a listing at any time, including while a report is reviewed.

Payments

Payments are processed by PayFast. OrangeGap does not see or store your card details. Funds are held by OrangeGap and paid out to sellers once an order is confirmed.

Collection and delivery

For orders placed through checkout, OrangeGap collects the item from the seller and delivers it to the buyer. Sellers must have the item ready and accurately described for collection; buyers should check the item on delivery and report any problem straight away.

Limits on our responsibility

OrangeGap takes reasonable care of an item while it is being collected and delivered, but is not liable for pre-existing damage, wear the seller failed to disclose, or delays outside our control, to the extent allowed by South African law. Any item exchanged directly between users outside of checkout is a private arrangement between them, and OrangeGap is not a party to it.

Changes

We may update these terms as OrangeGap develops. Continued use of the site means you accept the current version.

Privacy policy

Last updated September 2026.

What we collect

Your email address, university and campus, your delivery address and phone number if you buy something, and the listings, photos, events and messages you post.

Why we collect it

To run your account, show your listings, deliver orders and keep the platform safe. We do not sell your personal information.

Payments

Card and bank details are entered on PayFast's own page. OrangeGap never sees or stores them.

Analytics

We use Google Analytics to count visits and see which pages are used. It uses cookies and does not identify you by name.

Where it is stored

Your data is stored with Supabase. Passwords are hashed, and access rules stop users reading each other's private data.

Your rights under POPIA

You can ask to see, correct or delete your personal information at any time. Contact the OrangeGap team through the details on our social pages.
