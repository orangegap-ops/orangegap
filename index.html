About OrangeGap

Our mission

OrangeGap exists to close the gap between what students need and who has it. One platform for buying and selling with people you can trust, discovering campus events, and finding jobs, internships, bursaries and vacation work - all built around your specific campus and faculty.

Our story

OrangeGap started at NWU, built by a student who saw how scattered campus life had become - WhatsApp groups for selling textbooks, Facebook groups for events, noticeboards for jobs, none of it connected or verified. OrangeGap brings all of it into one trusted, campus-first platform.
